Haloscreed Battle Clade 3DP

FACTION PACKPriority Assets

Tech-Priests project fragments of their consciousness through the noosphere to override their thralls.

Detachment Rule

Noospheric Transference

In your Command phase, select one or more ADEPTUS MECHANICUS units, including embarked units: 1 at Incursion, 2 at Strike Force, or 3 at Onslaught. Until your next Command phase, they gain . Select one Override ability: Electromotive Energisation (+2" Move), Microactuator Bracing (+1 Toughness), Predation Protocols (eligible to charge after Advancing), or Muted Servomotors ().

Enhancements

Enhancement

Transoracular Dyad Wafers

CYBERNETICA DATASMITH model only. When attached to KASTELAN ROBOTS, models in that unit gain until the end of the battle. That unit cannot be selected for Noospheric Transference.

Enhancement

Cognitive Reinforcement

ADEPTUS MECHANICUS model only, excluding CYBERNETICA DATASMITH. The Conqueror and Protector Imperatives are both active for the bearer's unit.

Enhancement

Sanctified Ordnance

ADEPTUS MECHANICUS model only. Add 6" to the Range of ranged weapons equipped by models in the bearer's unit; tests for that unit can be re-rolled.

Enhancement

Inloaded Lethality

TECH-PRIEST DOMINUS or TECH-PRIEST MANIPULUS model only. Add 3 to the Attacks and 1 to the Damage characteristics of the bearer's melee weapons.

intervention to an extreme, exerting even finer control over their thralls. Projecting fragments of
their consciousness through the noospheric battle‑sphere, they override the autonomy of their
soldiery and inload tactical information, target coordinates and vector directives into cybernetic
minds. Such assistance is gratefully received by the Machine God’s faithful, who feel blessed by
the rush of divine wisdom and implanted knowledge.
