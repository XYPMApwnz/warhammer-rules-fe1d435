FAQ and Additional Datasheet Updates

Official update

Q: If changes a unit's Ballistic Skill to 3+ while Protector Imperative is active, in what order are the modifiers applied? A: First change Ballistic Skill to 3+, then improve it by 1 with Protector Imperative, for a final result of 2+ (assuming no other modifiers apply).
