Abilities

While this unit is within range of an objective marker you control, each time an attack with a Damage characteristic of 1 is allocated to a model in this unit, add 1 to any armour saving throw made against that attack.

Composition & Wargear

1 Heavy Intercessor Sergeant 4-9 Heavy Intercessors Every model is equipped with: bolt pistol; heavy bolt rifle; close combat weapon.

Wargear Options
  • For every 5 models in this unit, 1 Heavy Intercessor’s heavy bolt rifle can be replaced with 1 heavy bolter.
