Abilities

In your Movement phase, when this unit is set up on the battlefield using the Deep Strike ability, it can perform a meteoric descent. If it does, this unit can be set up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than 6" horizontally away from all enemy units, but until the end of the turn, it is not eligible to declare a charge.

Composition & Wargear

1 Inceptor Sergeant 2-5 Inceptors Every model is equipped with: assault bolters; close combat weapon.

Wargear Options
  • All models in this unit can each have their assault bolters replaced with 1 plasma exterminators.
