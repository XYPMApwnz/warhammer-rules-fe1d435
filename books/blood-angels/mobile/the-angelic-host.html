The Angelic Host2DP

SECONDARY CODEXDISRUPTION

Detachment Rule

At the end of your opponent's turn, select up to 1, 2 or 3 ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK units in Incursion, Strike Force or Onslaught battles respectively (excluding engaged units). Place them in Strategic Reserves; they must make an ingress move in your next Movement phase, including in your first turn. Restrictions: Your army can include BLOOD ANGELS units, but it cannot include any ADEPTUS ASTARTES units drawn from any other Chapter.

Enhancements

Enhancement · 15 pts

ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK model only. The bearer's melee weapons have [ANTI-CHAOS 5+] and [LANCE].

Enhancement · 20 pts

ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK model only. Improve the Armour Penetration characteristic of the bearer's weapons by 1, and the bearer has a Save characteristic of 2+.

Enhancement · 25 pts

ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK model only. In your opponent's Movement phase, if an enemy unit ends a move within 8\" of this unit and this unit is unengaged, this unit can make a Normal move of up to 6\".

Enhancement · 15 pts

ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK model only. In the Battle-shock step of your opponent's Command phase, each enemy unit (excluding MONSTERS and VEHICLES) within Engagement Range of the bearer must take a Battle-shock test.

Stratagems

Epic Deed
1CP

When
Any phase.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was just destroyed. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

Effect
Until the end of the battle, each time a friendly SANGUINARY GUARD unit attacks the enemy unit that destroyed your unit, re-roll the Hit and Wound rolls.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army selected as a target.

Effect
Until the attacking unit has finished, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of attacks targeting your unit by 1.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Start of the Fight phase.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit from your army.

Effect
Until the end of the phase, each enemy model within Engagement Range of your unit must target your unit with all of its attacks.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Fight phase.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit from your army that has not fought.

Effect
Until the end of the phase, melee weapons equipped by models in your unit have [LETHAL HITS] and [PRECISION].

Epic Deed
1CP

When
Your Movement phase.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit arriving using Deep Strike.

Effect
Your unit can be set up more than 6\" horizontally away from all enemy models.

Restrictions
That unit is not eligible to declare a charge in the same turn.

Epic Deed
1CP

When
Your Movement phase, just after an ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit Advances or Falls Back.

Target
That ADEPTUS ASTARTES JUMP PACK unit.

Effect
Until the end of the turn, your unit can shoot and declare a charge after Advancing or Falling Back.
