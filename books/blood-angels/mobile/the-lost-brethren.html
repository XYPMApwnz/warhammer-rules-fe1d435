The Lost Brethren2DP

SECONDARY CODEXPURGE THE FOE

Detachment Rule

Each time a DEATH COMPANY model from your army makes a melee attack, re-roll a Wound roll of 1 if that model's unit is below its Starting Strength; if that model's unit is Below Half-strength, you can re-roll the Wound roll instead. If you select this Detachment, DEATH COMPANY MARINES and DEATH COMPANY MARINES WITH BOLT RIFLES units from your army have the BATTLELINE keyword. This Detachment has the DOOMED tag. Restrictions: Your army can include BLOOD ANGELS units, but it cannot include any ADEPTUS ASTARTES units drawn from any other Chapter.

Enhancements

Enhancement · 25 pts

DEATH COMPANY model only. The first time the bearer is destroyed, at the end of the phase, roll one D6: on a 2+, set the bearer back up as close as possible to where it was destroyed and not within Engagement Range of enemy units, with 3 wounds remaining.

Enhancement · 20 pts

DEATH COMPANY model only. Once per battle, at the end of the Fight phase, if the bearer is within Engagement Range of three or more enemy models, the bearer can fight one additional time.

Enhancement · 15 pts

DEATH COMPANY model only. Once per battle, at the start of the Fight phase, if the bearer is within Engagement Range of an enemy CHARACTER unit, select one and roll six D6: for each 4+, one CHARACTER model in that unit suffers 1 mortal wound.

Enhancement · 10 pts

DEATH COMPANY model only. If the bearer is destroyed, until the end of your next turn, each time a friendly DEATH COMPANY model makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll.

Stratagems

Epic Deed
1CP

When
Any phase.

Target
One DEATH COMPANY unit from your army that was just destroyed while it was within range of an objective marker you controlled. You can use this Stratagem on that unit even though it was just destroyed.

Effect
That objective marker remains under your control until your opponent's Level of Control over it is greater than yours at the end of a phase.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

Target
One ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit from your army that was selected as a target.

Effect
Until the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, worsen the Armour Penetration characteristic of attacks targeting your unit by 1.

Epic Deed
1CP

When
Fight phase, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.

Target
One DEATH COMPANY unit from your army selected as a target.

Effect
Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit is destroyed, if that model has not fought this phase, roll one D6, adding 1 to the result if your unit is within 12\" of one or more friendly CHAPLAIN models; on a 4+, do not remove it from play. The destroyed model can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Fight phase.

Target
One DEATH COMPANY unit from your army, just before it Piles-in.

Effect
Until the end of the phase, Pile-in moves can be D3+3\"; if within 12\" of a friendly CHAPLAIN or below Starting Strength, they can be 6\" instead, subject to coherency and Engagement Range.

Battle Tactic
1CP

When
Fight phase.

Target
One DEATH COMPANY unit below Starting Strength that has not fought.

Effect
Until the end of the phase, improve Attacks, Strength and Armour Penetration of melee weapons by 1; unless within 12\" of a friendly CHAPLAIN, those weapons also have [HAZARDOUS].

Strategic Ploy
1CP

When
Your Movement phase, just after a DEATH COMPANY unit Advances.

Target
That DEATH COMPANY unit.

Effect
Until the end of the turn, your unit can declare a charge after Advancing. If within 12\" of a friendly CHAPLAIN or below Starting Strength, it can also shoot.
