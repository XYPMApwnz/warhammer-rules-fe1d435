Army Rules

Thrill Seekers

Army rule

If your Army Faction is EMPEROR’S CHILDREN, this unit is eligible to shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it Advanced or Fell Back, but when doing so:It cannot target a unit it was within Engagement Range of at the start of the turn.It cannot target a unit that was the target of another units charge or attack this phase.

Pact of Excess

Army rule

When mustering your army, unless specifically stated otherwise, you cannot select LEGIONS OF EXCESS as your Army Faction.
