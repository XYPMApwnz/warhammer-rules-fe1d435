Abilities

In your Shooting phase, this unit can select one visible enemy unit within 12". That enemy unit is prey-marked: - While a unit is prey-marked, that unit has +3" detection range.

Composition & Wargear

1 Kroot Kill-broker 9 Kroot Farstalkers 2 Kroot Hounds The Kroot Kill-broker is equipped with: Farstalker firearm; Kroot pistol; ritual blade. Every Kroot Farstalker is equipped with: Farstalker firearm; Kroot pistol; close combat weapon. Every Kroot Hound is equipped with: ripping fangs.

Wargear Options
  • The Kroot Kill-broker’s Farstalker firearm can be replaced with 1 T’au-tech rifle.
  • 1 Kroot Farstalker’s Farstalker firearm can be replaced with one of the following: 1 Dvorgite skinner 1 Londaxi tribalest
  • 1 Kroot Farstalker equipped with a Farstalker firearm can be equipped with 1 Pech’ra.
