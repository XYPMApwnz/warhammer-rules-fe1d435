Abilities

In your Shooting phase, after this model has shot, if it is not within Engagement Range of one or more enemy units, it can make a Normal move of up to 6". If it does, until the end of the turn, this model is not eligible to declare a charge.

Each time this model makes a ranged attack that hits an enemy unit, until the end of the turn, each time another KROOT model from your army makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll.

In your Shooting phase, this unit can select one visible enemy unit within 12". That enemy unit is prey-marked: - While a unit is prey-marked, that unit has +3" detection range.

Each time an attack is made with a [LANCE] weapon, if the attacking model’s unit made a charge move this turn, add 1 to the wound roll.

1 Kroot Lone-Spear This model is equipped with: Kroot long gun; close combat weapon; Kalamandra’s bite.

Wargear Options
  • This model’s Kroot long gun can be replaced with 1 blast javelin and 1 hunting javelin.
