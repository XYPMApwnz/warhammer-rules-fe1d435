# T'au Empire Compatible Rules parity

Scope: the 39 Codex datasheets only. The 4 Imperial Armour and 20 Legends datasheets remain published in the book but are intentionally absent from this snapshot, ledger and matrix.

## Inventory

- 7 Detachments: 3 from the official Faction Pack v1.1 and 4 Codex carry-forward detachments.
- 31 faction Stratagems: 7 official Faction Pack cards and 24 Codex carry-forward cards.
- 10 Core Stratagems from the local Core Rules render; no separate official Core/Universal Rules source PDF is stored locally.
- 23 Enhancements: 7 official Faction Pack cards and 16 Codex carry-forward cards.
- 2 official UPGRADE Enhancements.

## Source authority

The effective text uses the official Faction Pack v1.1 and its 25 update entries as overlays. That does not elevate the underlying community Codex transcription to primary-source authority. Wahapedia is used only for datasheet-to-Stratagem candidate associations, never as the canonical rule text.

The frozen Wahapedia snapshot contains 505 faction and 265 Core associations. Compared with the disabled legacy matcher, 62 disputed pairs were reviewed: 23 Wahapedia-only faction pairs, 26 matcher-only faction pairs and 13 matcher-only Core pairs. The ledger resolves all 62; unresolved count is zero.

## Enhancement ownership

The 23 explicit local owner contracts produce 93 owner associations in Codex scope: 89 standard Enhancement associations and 4 UPGRADE associations. EPIC HERO is excluded by every owner contract.

- Negation Emitters Upgrade: Stealth Battlesuits only.
- Unmasking Suite Upgrade: Ghostkeel Battlesuit, Pathfinder Team or Stealth Battlesuits.
- Both UPGRADE contracts retain `maxOwners: 3`, one Enhancement choice, and points paid per owner.

No owner is inferred from a possible attachment, a neighbouring roster unit, a datasheet name, or Detachment membership.
