Faction Pack v1.1 and MFM v1.1 are verified. Codex text remains a current community transcription and is identified as such.

Feed the Swarm Detachment Rule

Official update

In your Command phase, each HARVESTER unit from your army can Regenerate one friendly TYRANIDS unit that is within 6" of it. A unit can only be regenerated once per phase. Each time a unit regenerates, do one of the following: one model in that unit regains up to D3+1 lost wounds; or one destroyed INFANTRY model (excluding CHARACTERS) is returned to that unit with its full wounds remaining. If that unit is an ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit, up to 3 destroyed models are returned instead.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 19

Instinctive Defence Enhancement

Official update

TYRANIDS model only. While the bearer is within 6" of one or more friendly HARVESTER units, when you target this unit with the Heroic Intervention stratagem, that use is -1 CP. In addition, while the bearer is within 6" of one or more friendly HARVESTER units, models in the bearer’s unit have the Fights First ability.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 19

Enraged Behemoths Detachment Rule

Official update

Each time a TYRANIDS MONSTER model from your army makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll if that model’s unit is below its Starting Strength, and add 1 to the Wound roll as well if that model’s unit is Below Half-strength. In addition, while a TYRANIDS MONSTER unit from your army (excluding Battle-shocked units) is at its Starting Strength, add 2 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in that unit.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 19

Untrammelled Ferocity Stratagem, Effect Section

Official update

Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move, it can move through models (excluding TITANIC models) and sections of terrain features that are 4" or less in height. When doing so, it can move within Engagement Range of enemy models, but cannot end that move within Engagement Range of them. It can also move through sections of terrain features that are more than 4" in height, but if it does, after its unit has moved, roll one D6: on a 1, your unit is Battle-shocked.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 19

Reinforced Hive Node Stratagem, Effect Section

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 20

Do units that are repositioned by rules that do not specify that the unit being repositioned is first placed into Reserves (e.g. the Grey Knights Teleport Assault rule) need to take a Battle-shock test on a roll of 2+ if a TYRANIDS CHARACTER with the Hunting Grounds Enhancement is on the battlefield?

Official FAQ

Yes. Rules that are triggered by or apply to Reserves units are also triggered by and apply to a repositioned unit when it is set back up.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 21

Can I use the Rapid Regeneration Stratagem when an ability like Doombolt would inflict mortal wounds on a TYRANIDS unit from my army?

Official FAQ

No.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 21

When resolving a Mawloc’s Terror From the Deep ability, which units need to take a Battle-shock test: every unit within 12" of the Mawloc, or only those units for which a 5+ was rolled when resolving that ability?

Official FAQ

Only those units for which a 5+ was rolled.

Official Faction Pack v1.1 · p. 21

If my opponent uses a rule to place a unit into Strategic Reserves during the first battle round, and that rule states that the unit must arrive on the battlefield in the next battle round, what happens if a model from my army uses the Psychostatic Disruption Enhancement in the second battle round when that enemy Strategic Reserves unit is due to arrive?

Official FAQ

The ‘cannot arrive’ rule takes precedence over the rule stating that the unit ‘must arrive’ from Strategic Reserves. Roll one D6: on a 4+, that enemy unit cannot arrive on the battlefield this turn.
