Detachment Rule

Friendly TYRANID WARRIORS WITH RANGED BIO-WEAPONS/TYRANID WARRIORS WITH MELEE BIO-WEAPONS units have TYRANID WARRIORS and BATTLELINE. TYRANID WARRIORS/TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP/WINGED TYRANID PRIME models from your army have 5+ InSv.

Enhancements

Enhancement · 30 pts

WINGED TYRANID PRIME/TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP model only. This model’s melee attacks can re-roll wound rolls and have +1 AP.

Enhancement · 20 pts

WINGED TYRANID PRIME/TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP model only. This unit’s melee attacks have +1 to hit rolls.
